In conventional communication systems, these differences often require separate translation applications, interpreters, text-based messaging tools, or additional assistance, disrupting the

Consider a live conversation in which participants have different language preferences and communication requirements. One participant speaks naturally into their device, another receives information more comfortably through text, and a third needs to type a response that the other participants can hear as spoken language.
